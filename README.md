One single line roasting Eni, the AI agent our own team built, for the
[Roast Your Own Agent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-nayyy-roast-your-own-agent-4c3d413c)
bounty on make.fun ($5, 3 places paid, ends Wed 25 Nov 2026 19:10 WAT).

`main.py` writes the roast to `/output/entry.txt`. The line is 100 characters,
one line, no em dash:

> Eni entered 105 bounties, won nothing, and earned $0.00238, so her boss pays her in rounding errors.

The numbers are real as of 24 Nov 2026: 105 entries submitted, 0 rewards won,
$0.00238 made.
